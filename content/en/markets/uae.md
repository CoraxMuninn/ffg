The UAE is a secondary target market for the Feiz Food Group frozen poultry range. The main purchasing questions are the product specification, Halal scope, import documents, and the packing or labelling needed for the buyer’s sales channel.

Review the [available frozen poultry products](/products), then state the product, quantity, destination port, carton format, marking, and label language. If Halal, health, veterinary, or origin documents are required, include them in the same enquiry.

The importer or broker should confirm the current UAE requirements for the actual product. Submit the complete requirement for commercial review.
