Russia is a secondary target market for Feiz Food Group. A useful Russian-market enquiry should distinguish IQF from block-frozen product and define the specification, packing, Russian-language marking, and veterinary-document requirements.

For chicken feet, review the [Grade A/A+ IQF specification](/products/frozen-chicken-feet). For liver, gizzard, or heart, use the relevant [product page](/products) rather than applying the chicken-feet criteria to every poultry item.

The importer or customs representative should confirm current veterinary, sanitary, origin, and labelling requirements. Include the product, quantity, destination, and document list in the enquiry.
