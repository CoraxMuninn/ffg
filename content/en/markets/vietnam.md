Vietnam is the primary commercial market for Feiz Food Group. This page is written for importers, distributors, wholesalers, and processors comparing IQF frozen chicken feet for the Vietnamese market.

Begin with the [published frozen chicken feet specification](/products/frozen-chicken-feet): Grade A/A+, 35–55g average piece weight, 5–10% glaze, and storage at -18°C. A useful enquiry should also state quantity, packing, carton marking, and destination port.

Vietnamese import and document requirements can vary by product and import channel. Confirm the current position with the importer or customs representative, then include the agreed requirements in the quotation request.
