Thailand is a secondary target market for the Feiz Food Group frozen poultry range. Buyers comparing regional and international supply need offers built on the same product form, net weight, specification, packing, and delivery basis.

For frozen chicken feet, compare grade, average piece weight, glaze, visible condition, and carton uniformity against the [published specification](/products/frozen-chicken-feet). For other products, start from the [current product range](/products) and define the required criteria separately.

The importer should verify current Thai health, veterinary, origin, labelling, and market-access requirements. Include those details, the quantity, and destination port in the enquiry.
