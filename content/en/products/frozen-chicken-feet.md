Frozen chicken feet are the primary product in the Feiz Food Group range. The published specification is intended to give importers, distributors, wholesalers, and food processors a clear basis for an initial B2B enquiry.

## Published specification

The product is listed as Grade A/A+ and IQF frozen, with an average piece weight of 35–55g, a 5–10% glaze range, and storage at -18°C. Published appearance points are natural white colour, clean nails, no feathers, and no black spots.

The published requirements also include no blood, no contamination, and no off odour after defrost. The order specification should state how these points will be sampled and accepted.

## IQF form and handling

IQF freezes the pieces separately rather than as a solid block. This allows a buyer to inspect individual pieces, check the size range, or repack the product while it remains frozen.

The -18°C storage condition applies after freezing. Temperature records, inspection evidence, packing, labelling, shelf life, and shipment documents are not stated here as universal terms; they must be agreed for the order.

## Market and buyer context

Vietnam is the primary commercial market for this product. Buyers sourcing for Vietnam can review the [Vietnam market guidance](/markets/vietnam); the UAE, Russia, and Thailand are secondary target markets with different document and labelling considerations.

Importers, distributors, wholesalers, and processors should include quantity, destination port, packing or marking, and document requirements in the quotation request. Price, availability, minimum quantity, lead time, and shipping terms are confirmed for the enquiry.
