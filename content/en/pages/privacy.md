This Privacy Policy explains how Feiz Food Group handles information submitted through this website. It describes how the website is actually built and operated.

This is a business-to-business export website intended for importers, distributors, food processors, and other trade professionals.

## Information we collect

We collect information only when you choose to send it. The website does not create user accounts and does not require registration.

When you submit a quotation request through the contact form, the form collects:

- Contact name
- Company name
- Business email address
- Phone number
- Country or destination market
- Product of interest
- Quantity required
- Destination or port
- Packaging or specification notes
- Any additional requirements you enter in the message field

You can also contact us directly by email or telephone. In that case we receive whatever information you choose to include.

## How we use this information

We use what you submit for one purpose: to review your enquiry and respond to it with a quotation or a reply from our export team.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

## How your submission is handled

When you submit the form, your details are sent to our server, validated, and then delivered to our sales inbox as an email message. Email delivery is handled by Resend, an email delivery provider.

The website does not store your submission in a website database, and this website does not operate a customer relationship management (CRM) system. After delivery, your enquiry exists as an email in our business inbox and is handled by our export team.

Your business email address is set as the reply address on that email so our team can respond to you directly.

## Spam prevention and security

To keep the enquiry form usable and to prevent automated abuse, the website uses:

- Cloudflare Turnstile, a bot-protection check that runs when the form is submitted
- A submission rate limit that temporarily restricts how many requests can be sent in a short period
- Server-side validation of every submitted value

The rate limit operates on a short-lived, in-memory basis on the server and is not used to build a profile of you. The website is served over HTTPS.

## Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking scripts. Fonts are served from our own server rather than from an external font service.

The bot-protection check used on the enquiry form may set strictly necessary technical storage in your browser so that it can function. That is used only for security and abuse prevention, not to track your browsing.

## Third-party services

Two third-party services are involved in operating this website:

- **Resend** — delivers quotation request emails to our sales inbox
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** — provides the bot-protection check on the enquiry form

These providers process information only as needed for those functions.

## Retention

Quotation requests are received and kept as business correspondence in our email system for as long as needed to respond to the enquiry and to keep a normal commercial record of it. If you would like your enquiry removed, contact us using the details below.

## Your requests

If you want to know what enquiry information we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete your enquiry, contact us using the details below. We will respond to reasonable requests.

Depending on where your business is located, local data protection law may give you additional rights. Please tell us in your request if you are relying on a specific national or regional law so that we can respond appropriately.

## International handling

We are an export business and our contacts are international. Information you send may be received and read by our team and by the service providers listed above, which may be located in a different country from you.

## Children

This website is intended for business use. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website or our processes change. The date shown on this page indicates when it was last updated.
