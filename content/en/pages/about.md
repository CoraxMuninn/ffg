Feiz Food Group is a B2B frozen poultry exporter based in Iran. Our work is built around a focused product range: frozen chicken feet as the primary product, supported by chicken liver, gizzard, and heart.

We speak with importers, distributors, wholesalers, food processors, and trading companies. Those buyers need practical answers: what is the specification, which documents are required, how should the product be stored, and what information is needed for a useful quotation?

## Products and specifications

The published specification for our IQF frozen chicken feet covers Grade A/A+, average piece weight of 35–55g, 5–10% glaze, natural white colour, clean nails, no feathers, no black spots, and storage at -18°C. Product requirements are confirmed in writing for each enquiry.

The other products in the range are frozen chicken liver, gizzard, and heart. Their exact specification, packing, availability, and commercial terms are discussed against the buyer’s request rather than presented as fixed for every order.

See the [frozen poultry product range](/products) for the current items and published specifications.

## Quality and cold-chain planning

Quality discussions should be specific. Sample approval, size uniformity, glaze, broken pieces, carton sampling, defrost odour, temperature, pre-shipment inspection, and container-seal records are separate checkpoints—not one vague promise.

The relevant checks and evidence are agreed for the product and order. Our [quality-control overview](/quality-control) explains what each checkpoint is intended to verify, while the [export supply chain](/supply-chain) shows where freezing, storage, inspection, and loading sit in the process.

## Export documents

Document requirements depend on the destination and import channel. Health, veterinary, origin, Halal, HACCP-related, and ISO 22000-related documents or standards may be relevant, but the applicable set and issuing details must be confirmed for the shipment.

The [export documents page](/certifications) distinguishes document categories from proof issued for a specific lot. It does not present logos as evidence that every certificate is held by Feiz Food Group.

## Market focus

Vietnam is the primary commercial market for the business and receives dedicated Vietnamese content. The UAE, Russia, and Thailand are secondary target markets. These labels describe commercial focus; they are not statements about shipment volume, named customers, or fixed routes.

Read the [market guidance](/markets) for the product, packing, and document questions commonly raised for each destination.

## Starting an enquiry

A useful first message includes the product, quantity, destination country and port, required packing or marking, and any document requirements already known. Send those details through the [request-for-quote form](/contact) so the discussion can start with a defined requirement.
