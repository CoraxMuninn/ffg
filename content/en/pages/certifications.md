Frozen poultry shipments may require different documents depending on the product, destination, buyer, and import channel. The cards below identify categories that may enter that discussion.

They identify document categories, not certificates issued to Feiz Food Group or accepted in every destination. Current references, issuers, validity, and shipment-specific requirements are reviewed with the proposed order.

## Standards and shipment documents are different

HACCP and ISO 22000 are food-safety management frameworks. A health certificate, veterinary certificate, certificate of origin, or Halal document may instead be issued or required for a particular shipment. A logo or category name is not a substitute for the actual current document.

## Confirm the document set early

Before commercial terms are finalised, the buyer should identify the documents required by the destination authority, bank, customs broker, or sales channel. Feiz Food Group can then confirm what can be addressed for the proposed order.

Requirements can change, so this page is not customs, veterinary, or legal advice. Include the destination, product, and known document list in the [request for quote](/contact), and review the destination context on the [markets page](/markets).
