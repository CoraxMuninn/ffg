Our range covers four frozen poultry products. Frozen chicken feet are the main commercial focus; chicken liver, gizzard, and heart complete the current portfolio.

Each product page separates published facts from details that must be confirmed in a quotation. This lets procurement teams compare the right points without treating indicative website copy as a final contract specification.

## Understanding IQF product

IQF means individually quick frozen. Pieces are frozen separately rather than as one solid block, making them easier to inspect, portion, or repack while still frozen.

The listed products are presented in IQF form and at a storage temperature of -18°C. Any additional processing, packing, marking, or destination requirement should be written into the order specification.

## What to compare

For frozen chicken feet, the published points include grade, average piece weight, glaze range, colour, nail condition, feathers, black spots, and storage temperature. These details affect visual acceptance, usable net weight, handling, and resale or processing requirements.

Secondary products have shorter published specifications. We do not add unsupported size ranges, pack formats, shelf life, or quality grades to make those pages look more complete.
