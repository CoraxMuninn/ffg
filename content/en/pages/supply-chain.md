The export sequence shown here covers sourcing, processing, sorting, IQF freezing, cold storage, quality inspection, container loading, sea freight, and destination delivery.

The sequence is a process map. The supplier or farm, processing facility, ports, carrier, transit time, and delivery date are confirmed for each order.

## Product condition after freezing

Once product is frozen, the priority is to preserve its agreed condition. The documented storage temperature is -18°C from freezing onward. Cold storage and reefer-container transport are therefore part of the product specification, not just a logistics note.

Transfer points deserve particular attention because the product moves between processing, storage, and the container. Buyers should agree what temperature or loading evidence is required and who will review it.

## Inspection within the sequence

Inspection is shown before loading so the prepared lot can be checked against the agreed specification. Sample approval, glaze, size, broken-piece ratio, carton selection, temperature, and seal photographs are described separately on the [quality-control page](/quality-control).

## Booking-specific details

Ports, carrier, route, transit time, Incoterms, container type, and delivery scope depend on the booking and appear in the commercial documents.

Send the product, quantity, and destination port through the [RFQ form](/contact) so the applicable supply-chain and document requirements can be discussed for that enquiry.
