Use this form for a B2B product and price enquiry. Feiz Food Group does not publish fixed prices, stock, minimum quantities, or lead times; the first useful step is a clear requirement.

## Information to include

- **Product** — select frozen chicken feet, liver, gizzard, or heart.
- **Quantity** — state the tonnage or container requirement you are considering.
- **Destination country and port** — needed to discuss logistics and documents.
- **Specification** — include grade, size, glaze, packing, marking, or other required points.
- **Documents** — list any health, veterinary, origin, Halal, or other requirements already known.

## What the form does

Your enquiry is validated on the server and sent to the company inbox. It does not place an order, reserve stock, or create a contract. Product availability, specification, documentation, price, and commercial terms are confirmed separately in writing.
