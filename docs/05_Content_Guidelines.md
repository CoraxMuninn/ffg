# Feiz Food Group — Content Guidelines

## B2B Content System v2

## 1. Content Goal

Create natural, professional B2B content for an international frozen poultry exporter.

Content must build trust with

- Importers
- Distributors
- Wholesalers
- Food processing companies

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

Company Feiz Food Group

Industry Frozen Poultry Export

Primary Market Vietnam

Secondary Markets

- UAE
- Russia
- Thailand

---

## 3. Writing Style

Use

- Professional tone
- Natural human writing
- Clear international English
- Short paragraphs
- Strong information hierarchy
- Trust-focused language
- Practical business terminology

Avoid

- AI-sounding language
- Empty corporate phrases
- Consumer language
- Exaggerated promises
- Fake claims
- Keyword stuffing
- Unnecessary long paragraphs

---

## 4. Core Messaging

Every relevant page should reinforce appropriate aspects of

- Reliable supply
- Quality control
- Export capability
- Cold-chain management
- Bulk B2B supply
- Export documentation
- Long-term cooperation

Do not force every message onto every page.

Content should be contextually relevant.

---

## 5. Product Portfolio

### Primary

Frozen Chicken Feet

### Secondary

- Chicken Liver
- Chicken Gizzard
- Chicken Heart

Each product has an independent CMS-driven detail page.

Chicken Feet receives the strongest content depth.

---

## 6. Product Content

Where verified, Chicken Feet specifications include

- Grade A Grade A+
- IQF Frozen
- 35–55g average weight
- Natural white color
- Clean nails
- No black spots
- No bad odor after defrost
- 5–10% glaze
- No feathers
- No blood
- No contamination
- Storage temperature -18°C

Specifications must be editable through CMS.

Never create unsupported specifications for secondary products.

---

## 7. Supply Chain

Content may cover

1. Farm
2. Processing
3. Sorting
4. IQF Freezing
5. Cold Storage
6. Quality Inspection
7. Container Loading
8. Sea Shipping
9. Destination Delivery

Present only verified company practices as factual claims.

---

## 8. Quality Control

Relevant content includes

- Sample Approval
- Temperature Control
- Glaze Test
- Size Uniformity
- Broken Ratio Inspection
- Defrost Smell Test
- Random Carton Inspection
- Container Sealing Photos
- Pre-Shipment Inspection

---

## 9. Certifications & Compliance

Display

- HACCP
- ISO 22000
- Halal
- Health Certificate
- Veterinary Certificate
- Certificate of Origin

Certification content must be CMS-driven.

Do not create unsupported certification claims.

---

## 10. Trust Capability Features

Dynamic features may include

- Reliable Supply
- Strict Quality Control
- Cold-Chain Integrity
- Bulk Export
- Export Documentation
- Private Label
- Pre-Shipment Inspection
- Long-Term Cooperation

Use Lucide React icons.

These are business capabilities, not certifications.

---

## 11. Markets

Use one Markets page.

Include

- Vietnam
- UAE
- Russia
- Thailand

Vietnam receives primary emphasis.

Do not create individual market pages at this stage.

---

## 12. Contact

Support

- Office location
- Phone
- Email
- WhatsApp
- Telegram
- Zalo
- RFQ

Only display contact channels that are actually available.

---

## 13. RFQ Content

Primary CTA

Request a Quote

RFQ fields

Required

- Full Name
- Company Name
- Business Email
- Country
- Product
- Quantity
- Message

Optional

- Destination Port
- Packaging Specification

The form should feel quick and professional.

---

## 14. Homepage Messaging

Hero should communicate

- International exporter identity
- Reliable supply capability
- Professional B2B partnership

Primary CTA

Request a Quote

Homepage should guide the buyer through

Hero → Products → Product confidence → Quality Trust → Supply Chain → Markets → RFQ

Avoid overwhelming the buyer with excessive copy.
